# Bike maintenance

## Tyres

For the road bike on 28 mm tyres, pump to 80 to 90 psi for a rider of about 70 kg; add 5 psi for every 10 kg more. The hybrid commuter on 40 mm tyres runs at 55 to 65 psi. Check pressures every week, as tyres slowly lose air even without a puncture.

## Chain

Clean and lube the chain every 300 km, and after every ride in the rain. Put one drop of lube on each roller, spin the pedals for a minute, then wipe off the excess, because lube left on the outside attracts grit.

Replace the chain when a chain checker shows 0.5% wear. Leaving a worn chain on wears out the cassette and chainrings too, which costs far more than a chain.

## Brakes

Rim brake pads need replacing when the grooves in the pad have worn away. Disc brake pads need replacing when the pad material is thinner than 1.5 mm; the rotors themselves should be replaced when they wear below the minimum thickness stamped on them.

If the brakes squeal, clean the rotors with isopropyl alcohol and make sure no lube has got onto them.
